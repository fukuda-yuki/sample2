superiority requirement is adopted.** This supersedes the v1 proposal for
future amendment; the v1 proposal itself remains historical and unchanged.

Suggested insertion:

> The bounded MS1 catalog modernization extension examines how initial source
> presentation affects all-assigned total provider tokens, all-requirement
> fulfillment and subsequent agent behavior under fixed task, model, agent
> and retrieval conditions. Its primary outputs are arithmetic mean token
> differences, all-29 pass-rate differences and their uncertainty. Relative
> quality superiority may be reported when supported, but is not required to
> obtain useful comparative knowledge. Without a justified quality-loss
> boundary, quality maintenance remains unestablished and the quality interval
> and its limits must be reported.
>
> A verified requirement failure determines binary all-29 failure even when
> other checks are unfinished; coverage remains separately visible. Invalid
> model/input identity blocks shared inference, evaluation identity faults
> block quality inference, and true usage missingness blocks the all-assigned
> token interval. Known totals are not vetoed solely by unfinished quality
> inspection. Missingness is not zero-filled or replaced by successful or
> convenient complete cases.
>
> The accepted technical pilot is prior evidence, outside the future
> comparison population. The v1 320-pair calculation is retained as design
> history, not an adopted execution count. The v2 resource comparison lists
> sensitivity, precision, missingness and retention/time implications for
> existing candidate sizes. Numerical allocation is deferred until usable
> provider capacity, hours and storage reserve are documented and the selected
> uncertainty is useful. No new model acquisition has been authorized or
> performed by this revision.

Link to the [v2 plan](ms1-catalog-comparison-v2-plan.md), machine plan, resource
comparison and validation rather than duplicating the executable specification.
No new experiment/result record should imply actual acquisition. Human review
remains `not_run`; evaluator acceptance and the technical pilot remain accepted.
